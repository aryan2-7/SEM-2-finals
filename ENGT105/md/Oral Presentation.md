# Introduction
 An oral presentation is a formal method of delivering information or ideas to an audience by speaking.
 Preparing and presenting content in a structured manner to inform, persuade, or entertain listeners.
 A verbal communication process where a speaker conveys a message, knowledge, or ideas to an audience.
 Can be conducted in various settings such as academic, professional, or informal contexts and usually involves the speaker using spoken words, often supported by visual aids like slides, charts, or multimedia.

# Listening skills
- No communication occurs if you don’t listen to the others.
- Collaboration demands that all the voices to be heard.
 - Successful workplace value’s everyone’s input.
Barriers to Active listening:
* Multitasking
* Preconceived notions
* Focusing on your response
* Interrupting the speaker
* External distraction

# Tips for Effective Listening:
- Stop What you are doing, concentrate on the task at hand.
- Do not talk. Let the speaker have his or her say.
- Make eye contact with the speaker.
- Take notes to stay focused and for future references.
- Ask questions for clarification and to stay engaged.
- Be objective, open to alternatives, and willing to accept difference of option.
- Control your reaction. Even a short delay inn response will let you digest the issues, consider other points of view, organized thoughts, and respond professionally.

# The Importance of Oral communication
1. Oral communication ensures clarity by simplifying complex technical jargon, making it easier for non-technical audiences to understand.
2. It facilitates collaboration by enabling teams to share knowledge and align on technical goals effectively.
3. Through oral communication, real-time feedback can be obtained, allowing for immediate adjustments to technical content.
4. It builds credibility and rapport, demonstrating the speaker's expertise and fostering trust among stakeholders.

<!-- Slide number: 7 -->
# The Importance of Oral communication
5. Oral communication aids decision-making by presenting technical details concisely and providing actionable insights.
6. It bridges cultural and professional gaps, making technical information relatable and accessible to diverse audiences.
7. Effective oral discussions support problem-solving by fostering dynamic idea exchanges and collaborative solutions.
8. Oral communication reinforces written materials by elaborating on or defending points made in technical documents, ensuring comprehensive understanding.

# Tips for Using PowerPoint:
1. Create optimal contrast.
2. Choose an easy eye-to-read font size and styles.
3. Limit the text per slide.
4. Use headings for readability.
5. Use emphasis techniques.
6. Prepare handouts.
7. Avoid reading your screens to your audiences.
8. Elaborate on each screen.
9. Leave enough time for questions and comments.
