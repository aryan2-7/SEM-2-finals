# Examine your purposes
Before writing, need to know why are you communicating?
Internal Motivation:
If you have decided to write on your own solidarity that is internal motivation.
Ex: You write a letter of inquiry to help you perform your job well, You call a meeting to report your findings
External Motivation:
If someone else has requested for the correspondence that is external motivation.
Ex: your boss asks to write the report, customers ask you to respond to the complaint letter

- Move around information (Cut and paste)
       to ensure that your most important ideas are emphasized.
- Reformat to ensure reader-friendly ease of access.
- Enhance the tone and style of the text.
- Correct any errors to ensure any grammar and content.

# Importance of Proofreading
1. Accuracy and Precision : Technical writing often includes data, figures, and instructions. Errors in these can mislead the audience, causing confusion or incorrect actions.
For example, a mistake in a user manual could lead to improper product use, potentially causing harm.
2. Clarity : Even small errors, like types or grammatical mistakes, can make a document harder to understand. Proofreading ensures the message is conveyed clearly and unambiguously.
3. Professionalism and Credibility : Mistakes in technical documents can challenge trust in the author or organization.
Proofreading maintains a professional tone, boosting credibility with the audience.
4. Compliance and Legal Safety : In regulated industries like healthcare, engineering, or aviation, errors in technical writing could result in legal repercussions or non-compliance with standards.
5. Efficiency : Proofreading ensures the final document is polished and ready for its intended purpose, minimizing the need for revisions and saving time in the long run.

Date: April 3, 2012To: William HuddlestonFrom: Julie SchopperSubject: Feedback on Training Budget Solutions
Dear Bill,
Our recent training budget has exceeded projections, and we need to address this issue promptly. My project team has developed a few potential solutions, and we’d appreciate your review and input. Below are the proposed options:
Reduce the Number of Training Classes:
Decrease the number of classes while increasing the number of participants per session.
This approach would maintain participant income but reduce costs by lowering trainer salaries and benefits.
A potential downside could be a decline in training effectiveness due to a higher trainer-to-participant ratio.
Outsource Training Services:
Transition to outsourcing all training sessions.
This would eliminate the need for in-house trainers, resulting in significant savings on salaries and benefits.
Outsourcing could also allow us to maintain the current number of sessions and a low trainer-to-participant ratio.
Please share your thoughts on these options at your earliest convenience. Your feedback is essential for us to move forward, so even if you are busy, a quick response would be greatly appreciated.
Looking forward to hearing from you soon.
Best regards,Julie Schopper
