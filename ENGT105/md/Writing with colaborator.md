# What?
Writing with collaborators
collaborative writing :  process in which two or more people work together to plan, write, revise, and edit a document.
Instead of one person completing the entire writing task, responsibilities are shared among team members.

Definition
According to technical communication experts, collaborative writing occurs when multiple writers contribute to a single document and share responsibility for its content.

<!-- Slide number: 3 -->
# Why?
In professional and academic settings, many documents are too large or complex for one person to complete alone. Collaboration helps:
Combine different expertise and perspectives
Improve the quality of writing
Save time by dividing tasks
Increase accuracy through peer review
Enhance creativity and problem-solving

1. Planning
Define the purpose and audience.

Members read each other's work.

# Models of Collaborative writing
| Model | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Single Author | One person writes, others review. |
| Sequential Writing | One person writes first, then passes it to others. |
| Parallel Writing | Different sections are written simultaneously by different members. |
| Reactive Writing | Team members work together and make changes continuously. |

# Advantages and Challenges
Advantages
Produces higher-quality documents
Shares workload
Brings diverse knowledge and skills
Encourages teamwork and communication
Reduces errors through multiple reviews

Challenges
 Conflicting ideas and writing styles
Communication difficulties
Unequal participation among members
Scheduling problems
 Version control issues
