# What is Digital Literacy?
Ability to use digital tools effectively and responsibly.
Combines technical, cognitive, and communication skills.
Involves online reading, writing, creating, and critical thinking.

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 3 -->
# Why Digital Literacy ?
Access and navigate digital platforms and tools efficiently.
Evaluate the credibility and accuracy of online information.
Communicate effectively and ethically in virtual environments.
Maintain cybersecurity and protect personal data.
Use digital tools creatively to solve problems or produce content.

<!-- Slide number: 4 -->
# Why Digital Literacy Matters for Today’s Youth?
Educational Opportunities: e-learning opportunities, interactive tools, and virtual classrooms.
Career Preparedness: Computer literacy skills, familiarity with digital collaboration tools, and proficiency in specialized software. meet these expectations and remain competitive in the job market.
Social Connectivity and Advocacy: Digital platforms enable youth to connect, collaborate, and advocate for causes that matter to them. Social media, blogs, and other tools empower them to express their voices and build networks.
Critical Thinking and Resilience: By learning to evaluate information online, youth can discern facts from misinformation, a vital skill in an era of “fake news.” Moreover, digital literacy teaches adaptability in a rapidly changing tech landscape.
Bridging Inequalities: Access to digital tools and literacy can help reduce socio-economic inequalities by providing young people with opportunities to learn, innovate, and participate in the global economy.

# What is Visual Communication?
Using visuals to convey ideas or data
Includes images, videos, infographics, presentations
Enhances clarity, retention, and engagement

<!-- Slide number: 13 -->
# Visual Literacy Skills:
Understanding design elements: color, contrast, balance
Creating appealing layouts using digital tools (Canva, PPT, Adobe)
Interpreting data through graphs and charts
Ethical use of visuals (attribution and copyright)
