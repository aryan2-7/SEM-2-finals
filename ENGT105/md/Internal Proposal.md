# What is a Proposal?
A proposal is a formal document used to persuade readers to accept an idea, service, product, or solution.
It explains:
A problem or need
Suggested solutions
Benefits of the proposal
Goal: to convince the audience to take action

<!-- Slide number: 3 -->
# Why Write a Proposal?
Main Purpose
To sell an idea persuasively
To solve organizational problems
To request approval, funding, or support
Example Situations
Building a new office facility
Improving employee recruitment
Expanding company services
Introducing new technology

<!-- Slide number: 4 -->
# Types of Proposals
1. Internal Proposal
Written within an organization
Sent to managers or company executives
Examples
Purchasing new laptops and smartphones
Changing insurance plans
Employee software training

<!-- Slide number: 5 -->
# External Proposal
Written for clients outside the company
Used to sell products or services
Example
Selling biotechnology software
Offering consulting and training services

<!-- Slide number: 6 -->
# Requests for Proposals (RFPs)
What is an RFP?
A formal request asking companies to submit proposals
Common Users
Government agencies
Hospitals
City councils
Businesses
Examples
Road repair projects
Internet security systems
