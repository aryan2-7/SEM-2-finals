## THE BIG ONE: TNB FRAME

You want the entire pipeline to become automatic.

Given:

r(t)

### 1. Velocity

r′(t)

### 2. Speed

∣r′(t)∣

### 3. Unit tangent

T=∣r′∣r′​​

### 4. Principal normal

N=∣T′∣T′​​

### 5. Binormal

B=T×N​

### 6. Curvature

κ=∣r′∣3∣r′×r′′∣​​

### 7. Torsion

Know the formula:

τ=∣r′×r′′∣2(r′×r′′)⋅r′′′​​
