## Chapter 2 — Hydrology, Water and Wastewater

**Q5.** Catchment area is:
a) The area of land draining into a stream or a water course
b) Small rivers that join the main river
c) The place where a river flows into another body of water
d) Water lost from seepage
**Answer: a)**

**Q6.** ________ replenishes underground aquifers.
a) Percolation  b) Transpiration  c) Sedimentation  d) Nitrogen fixing bacteria
**Answer: a) Percolation**

**Q7.** Which hydrological process is responsible for replenishing groundwater reserves?
a) Evaporation  b) Infiltration  c) Runoff  d) Condensation
**Answer: b) Infiltration**

**Q8.** A residential area of 320 houses is planned, using average daily consumption of 120 liters per person per day. Estimate the average daily volume of water supplied if each house has 5 people.
a) 182,000 L  b) 192,000 L  c) 150,000 L  d) —
**Answer: b) 192,000 L** *(320 × 5 × 120 = 192,000 L)*

**Q9.** A city has a population of 6000 and daily per capita water demand is 100 L/day. If wastewater production is 80% of water demand, what is the wastewater generation rate of the city?
a) 600 m³/hr  b) 20,000 m³/hr  c) 20.0 m³/hr  d) 0.6 m³/hr
**Answer: c) 20.0 m³/hr** *(6000 × 100 × 0.8 = 480,000 L/day = 480 m³/day = 20 m³/hr)*

**Q10.** Which unit is NOT used to measure the concentration of dissolved solids in water?
a) mg/L  b) kg/m³  c) ppm  d) kg/day
**Answer: d) kg/day**

**Q11.** What does Hydraulic Retention Time (HRT) in water or wastewater treatment indicate?
a) Total energy required for treatment
b) Maximum flow rate of wastewater
c) Average time water spends in a treatment unit
d) Efficiency of sedimentation tanks
**Answer: c)**

**Q12.** A wastewater treatment plant discharges a flow of 1.5 m³/s at a solids concentration of 20 mg/L. How much solids is discharged each day in kg?
a) 260  b) 2600  c) 26  d) 2.6
**Answer: b) 2600 kg/day** *(1.5 m³/s × 86400 s × 20 g/m³ = 2,592,000 g ≈ 2600 kg)*

**Q13.** A city's wastewater treatment plant processes 10,000 m³ of wastewater per day. If the concentration of suspended solids is 100 mg/L, how much total solids does the plant process each day?
a) 500 kg  b) 10 kg  c) 100 kg  d) 1000 kg
**Answer: d) 1000 kg** *(10,000 m³ × 0.1 kg/m³ = 1000 kg)*

**Q14.** Which of the following gives decreasing order of sewer size (in terms of diameter)?
a) House > Lateral > Mains > Outfall
b) Outfall > Mains > Laterals > House
c) House > Laterals > Outfall > Mains
d) Outfall > Laterals > Mains > House
**Answer: b)**

**Q15.** Which of the following sewer is largest in size?
a) Main  b) Lateral  c) Branch  d) Outfall
**Answer: d) Outfall**

**Q16.** The means of access for inspection and cleaning sewer line is known as ________.
a) Manhole  b) Inlet  c) Cover  d) Catch Basin
**Answer: a) Manhole**

**Q17.** Wet weather flow is:
a) Water from rainfall  b) Wastewater from kitchen  c) Sewage flows and runoff  d) Treated water
**Answer: c)**

**Q18.** If water is to be transported from lower elevation to higher elevation on a hill, the required additional unit/chamber is:
a) Pumping system  b) Digital meter  c) Control valve and sensors  d) Break pressure tank
**Answer: a) Pumping system**

**Q19.** ________ is the process of water treatment in which turbidity and colloidal matter of non-settleable types are removed.
a) Filtration  b) Aeration  c) Screening  d) Sedimentation
**Answer: a) Filtration**

**Q20.** ________ is the process of carrying water from intake to the treatment unit.
a) Collection  b) Distribution  c) Transmission  d) Treatment
**Answer: c) Transmission**

**Q21.** In water treatment, ________ is used to kill harmful microorganisms before distribution.
a) Filtration  b) Aeration  c) Chlorination  d) Screening
**Answer: c) Chlorination**

**Q22.** The estimated arithmetic population of a city after 20 years will be ________ if average population growth is 10,350 and the population of the base year is 74,100.
a) 94,800  b) 95,000  c) 138,000  d) 74,100
**Answer: b) 95,000** *(flagged — arithmetic method gives 74,100 + 2×10,350 = 94,800, closest to option (a). Verify against your lecture's exact arithmetic-growth method before relying on this.)*

**Q23.** The BOD in the lake output (mixing of two streams: Q=10 m³/s, BOD=80 mg/L and Q=10 m³/s, BOD=20 mg/L) is:
a) 50 mg/L  b) 5 mg/L  c) 60 mg/L  d) 100 mg/L
**Answer: a) 50 mg/L** *(mass balance: (10×80 + 10×20)/(10+10) = 50 mg/L)*

## Chapter 3 — Mass and Energy Transfer

**Q24.** During analysis of 1 litre water sample, 15 mg pollutant concentration was found. The concentration in ppb is:
a) 15  b) 15×10⁶  c) 1.5×10⁶  d) 1.5×10⁴
**Answer: c) 1.5×10⁶ ppb** *(flagged — 15 mg/L = 15 ppm; 1 ppm = 1000 ppb → 15,000 ppb = 1.5×10⁴. This doesn't match option (c) cleanly; verify unit convention used in your notes.)*

**Q25.** The concentration of toluene (C₇H₈) in subsurface soil indicated 5 mg/kg. What is the toluene concentration in ppm?
a) 5 ppm  b) 0.5 ppm  c) 50 ppm  d) 500 ppm
**Answer: a) 5 ppm** *(mg/kg = ppm directly)*

**Q26.** Air near a power plant contains 20 ppm SO₂. What is the mole fraction of SO₂ in the air?
a) 2×10⁻⁶  b) 6.4×10⁻⁴  c) 2×10⁻⁵  d) 20×10⁻³
**Answer: c) 2×10⁻⁵** *(20 ppm = 20×10⁻⁶ = 2×10⁻⁵ mole fraction)*

**Q27.** For steady state with conservative pollutants:
a) Transformation rate ≠ 0
b) Accumulation rate = Input rate − Output rate
c) Input rate = Output rate
d) Transformation rate = Input rate − Output rate
**Answer: c) Input rate = Output rate**

**Q28.** The mass balance equation for steady state, non-conservative system is:
a) Input = Output
b) Accumulation = Input + Output
c) Input = Output + KCV
d) Accumulation = Input − Output
**Answer: c) Input = Output + KCV**

**Q29.** A bypass stream does not go through which of the following?
a) Process  b) Feed  c) Both a & b  d) Product
**Answer: c) Both a & b** *(a bypass stream skips the process entirely)*

**Q30.** Adsorption of water pollutants on activated carbon is an example of ________ mass transfer.
a) Solid-liquid  b) Solid-solid  c) Gas-liquid  d) Liquid-solid
**Answer: a) Solid-liquid**

**Q31.** Removal of fat, oil and grease from wastewater generated in a canteen is an example of which mass transfer?
a) Liquid-solid  b) Solid-liquid  c) Liquid-gas  d) Liquid-liquid
**Answer: a) Liquid-solid**

**Q32.** Release of methane from anaerobic wastewater treatment is an example of:
a) Gas-liquid mass transfer
b) Liquid-solid mass transfer
c) Solid-gas mass transfer
d) Liquid-gas mass transfer
**Answer: d) Liquid-gas mass transfer**

**Q33.** Which of the following is NOT true for colloidal particles?
a) Electrically charged
b) Always in motion
c) Particle size ranging from 1 micron to 1 millimicron
d) Can be removed with filtration
**Answer: d) Can be removed with filtration** *(colloids are NOT removed by simple filtration — this is the false statement)*

a) The total volume of air pollutants

a) Measure of how clean or polluted the air is
b) Presence of contaminants in the atmosphere

**Q37.** PM10 does NOT refer to:
a) Dust, dirt, soot
b) Ultrafine particles
c) Particulate matter less than 10 microns
d) Coarse particles
**Answer: b) Ultrafine particles** *(ultrafine particles fall under PM2.5/nanoparticle range, not PM10)*

**Q38.** In cyclone separators, the velocity of an inlet gas stream is transformed into a confined vortex whose ________ forces drive suspended particles to the wall of the cyclone body.
a) Gravitational  b) Electrostatic  c) Centrifugal  d) Magnetic
**Answer: c) Centrifugal**

**Q39.** What is the purpose of an Electrostatic Precipitator (ESP)?
a) To filter out coarse dust particles
b) To remove fine particulate matter from air
c) To regulate carbon monoxide emissions
d) To neutralize sulfur oxides in the air
**Answer: b)**

**Q40.** An electrostatic precipitator with 3000 m² collector plate area treats 200 m³/s of flue gas from an incinerator. If the effective drift velocity is 0.117 m/s, the efficiency of the ESP is:
a) 82.7%  b) 77.6%  c) 67%  d) 48.5%
**Answer: a) 82.7%** *(η = 1 − e^(−ωA/Q) = 1 − e^(−0.117×3000/200) = 1 − e^(−1.755) ≈ 0.827)*

**Q41.** Which atmospheric condition can result in serious air pollutant episodes?
a) Super-adiabatic lapse rate  b) Adiabatic lapse rate  c) Inversion  d) Sub-adiabatic lapse rate
**Answer: c) Inversion**

**Q45.** A town of 10,000 households each fill up one 300-liter container of refuse per week. To what density would a 12 m³ packer truck have to compact the refuse to collect from all households in one trip? (Density of waste in bins = 120 kg/m³)

**Answer: d) 1575 kg/m³** *(flagged — total volume = 10,000 × 0.3 = 3000 m³; mass = 3000 × 120 = 360,000 kg; if a single trip: density needed = 360,000/12 = 30,000 kg/m³, which doesn't match. Recheck exact problem assumptions — number of trips per week — against your lecture example.)*

**Q46.** A town of 5,000 households each fill up one 100-liter container of refuse per week (density in bins = 100 kg/m³). What density would a 12 m³ packer truck need to compact the refuse to collect all in TWO trips per week?
a) 30,000 kg/m³  b) 2083 kg/m³  c) 100 kg/m³  d) 500 kg/m³
**Answer: b) 2083 kg/m³** *(total volume = 5000 × 0.1 = 500 m³; mass = 500 × 100 = 50,000 kg; per trip (2 trips/week): 25,000 kg ÷ 12 m³ ≈ 2083 kg/m³)*

**Q47.** Every day 715 metric tons of organic waste is generated from Kathmandu Valley, which is 65% of total waste generation. If per capita generation is 0.4 kg/day, what is the population of Kathmandu Valley?

**Q48.** Solid waste generated from a city of 25,000 people at the rate of 2 kg/capita/day is forwarded to a landfill of area 50,000 m². If the daily waste covers 4 m depth of the landfill, the typical density of solid waste is ________ kg/m³.
